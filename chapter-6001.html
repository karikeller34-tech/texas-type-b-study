General provisions and agency powers

TEX. INS. CODE §6001.001 · PURPOSE
Q. What two kinds of work does Chapter 6001 regulate, and what does it prohibit?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. It regulates portable extinguishers: lease, sell, install, service; and fixed systems: plan, certify, install, service. It prohibits portable extinguishers, fixed systems, or equipment not listed by a department-approved testing laboratory.
Memory hook: Portable = L-S-I-S. Fixed adds two front-end jobs: Plan + Certify, then I-S. Think: “portable is the little list; fixed needs a plan and a certificate.”
Watch for: Do not say “all unlisted equipment is allowed if it works.” The statute’s point is the approved laboratory listing. Gabe’s birth-year anchor: enacted 2007, effective 2009—he was born in the middle, in 2008.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.002 · DEFINITIONS
Q. What does a monthly portable-extinguisher inspection check?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. Three things: it is in its designated location; it has not been actuated or tampered with; and it has no obvious damage or condition that may prevent proper operation.
Memory hook: Picture a three-finger check: Place – Pin – Physical. Place = where it belongs; Pin = not pulled/tampered; Physical = not damaged.
Watch for: “Inspection” is not the same as “service.” Service means charging, filling, maintaining, recharging, refilling, repairing, or testing.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.051 · ADMINISTRATION OF CHAPTER
Q. Who administers Chapter 6001, and who may issue necessary rules?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. The department shall administer Chapter 6001. The commissioner may issue necessary rules through the State Fire Marshal.
Memory hook: Say: Department drives; Commissioner writes; Fire Marshal carries it out.
Watch for: Trap word: department shall administer; commissioner may issue rules.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.052 · ADOPTION OF RULES
Q. Name the four kinds of standards the commissioner may use when adopting necessary rules.
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. The commissioner may use standards published by NFPA, recognized by federal law/regulation, published by a nationally recognized standards-making organization, or contained in manufacturer installation manuals.
Memory hook: N-F-N-M: NFPA, Federal, National organization, Manufacturer. “NFPA follows federal/national/manufacturer.”
Watch for: May use those standards. But the commissioner shall adopt/administer rules essentially necessary to protect life/property and shall prescribe application and qualification requirements.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.053 · RULES RESTRICTING ADVERTISING OR COMPETITIVE BIDDING
Q. What is the only reason the commissioner may restrict advertising or competitive bidding?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. Only to prohibit false, misleading, or deceptive practices.
Memory hook: F-M-D is the only door. If it is not False, Misleading, or Deceptive, the door stays shut.
Watch for: Even under F-M-D rules, the commissioner may not restrict the advertising medium, personal appearance/voice, size/duration, or use of a trade name.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.054 · GENERAL POWERS AND DUTIES OF DEPARTMENT
Q. What does the department evaluate and what credentials may it issue under Chapter 6001?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. It evaluates firms applying for registration and firms seeking testing-lab approval. It issues qualifying registration certificates, licenses, apprentice permits, and hydrostatic-testing authorizations.
Memory hook: Evaluate firms; issue four: R-L-P-H (Registration, License, Permit, Hydro).
Watch for: This is about the department’s gatekeeper job, not the State Fire Marshal’s exam job.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.055 · FEES
Q. What are the statutory maximum fees for firm, branch, DOT hydro, employee license, apprentice permit, and exam/reexam?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. Firm $450 initial / $300 annual renewal; branch $100 / $100; DOT hydro $250 / $150; employee license $70 / $50; apprentice $30; if not run by a testing service, initial exam $30, reexam $20; duplicate/change up to $20.
Memory hook: Read it as a descending ladder: 450–300, 100–100, 250–150, 70–50, 30–20. Firm is biggest; a reexam is the final $20.
Watch for: These are statutory maximums, not automatically today’s charged fees. “Annual” applies to the listed renewal fees in this statute.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.056 · DEPOSIT IN OPERATING ACCOUNT
Q. Where does money collected under Chapter 6001 go?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. It is paid to the department and deposited in the state treasury to the credit of the TDI operating account for administering the chapter—except penalties and monetary forfeitures.
Memory hook: Fees fuel the office; penalties do not.
Watch for: The trap is the exception: do not say all money goes to the operating account.

Registration, licenses, permits, and renewal

TEX. INS. CODE §6001.151 · FIRM REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE REQUIRED
Q. What must a firm have before it may install/service portable extinguishers or plan/certify/install/service fixed systems?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. A registration certificate issued by the department.
Memory hook: Firm first. Before work, the business needs the firm certificate.
Watch for: Do not confuse the firm’s certificate with the individual employee’s license.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.152 · BRANCH OFFICE REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE REQUIRED
Q. When does a separate office need a branch-office registration certificate?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. Each separate office location of a registered firm, other than the location on the firm certificate, must have a branch certificate. Before issuing it, the department must determine the branch is part of a registered firm.
Memory hook: New address, new branch paper. But only after it is tied back to the registered firm.
Watch for: The office must be separate; this is not about every truck or employee.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.153 · HYDROSTATIC TESTING; REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE REQUIRED
Q. What must a firm have to hydrostatically test DOT-specification extinguishers?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. It must follow DOT compressed-gas-cylinder procedures and hold an State Fire Marshal registration certificate authorizing hydrostatic testing. The individual’s license must show that authority. Non-DOT testing follows NFPA recommendations.
Memory hook: DOT = DOT rules + DOT certificate + DOT authority on the license. No DOT? Think NFPA.
Watch for: DOT versus non-DOT is the entire trap. Do not use NFPA as the answer for DOT cylinders.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.154 · REQUIRED INSURANCE COVERAGE FOR REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE
Q. What insurance and minimum limits are required before a registration certificate can issue?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. General liability insurance with products and completed operations coverage, at least $100,000 each occurrence combined single limit for bodily injury/property damage and $300,000 aggregate per policy year, unless lawfully changed after notice/hearing.
Memory hook: 100 per fire, 300 for the year. And remember P + CO: Products + Completed Operations.
Watch for: Failure to maintain it can support denial, suspension, or revocation after notice and opportunity for hearing.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.155 · EMPLOYEE LICENSE REQUIRED
Q. Who needs an employee license before doing covered extinguisher work?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. Except for §6001.156 exemptions, every individual other than an apprentice must be licensed before portable work; preengineered fixed-system install/service/certification; or planning, supervising, servicing, or certifying other fixed-system installation. The license holder must be an employee or agent of a registered firm.
Memory hook: Person license + firm home. A licensee cannot float alone; he works for a registered firm.
Watch for: This statute says employee license, not just “firm registration.” It also separates apprentices.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.156 · ACTIVITIES NOT REGULATED BY CHAPTER
Q. What is the building-owner exemption?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. A building owner, the owner’s managing agent, or their employee may install portable fire extinguishers in the owner’s building.
Memory hook: Owner + own building + portable only. Three locks on the exception.
Watch for: Do not stretch it to fixed systems, service for pay, inspections generally, or another person’s building. Other listed exemptions include manufacturer-before-first-sale, own trained firm personnel, trained contract inspection, NFPA water sprinklers, sales-only, free trained fire-department service, a supervised/certified contract installation, and an engineer acting solely professionally.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.157 · LICENSE EXAMINATION
Q. Who establishes and administers the license exam, and what may a testing service do?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. The State Fire Marshal shall establish scope/type and examine applicants. The Marshal may administer it or contract with a testing service for development, scheduling, sites, grading, reporting, analysis, and other administration; the service may correspond with applicants, collect a reasonable fee, or test at a specified place/time.
Memory hook: Marshal owns the test; PSI can run the test. Think seven PSI chores: develop, schedule, site, grade, report, analyze, admin.
Watch for: “May contract” does not mean PSI creates the legal scope on its own.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.158 · EXAMINATION RESULTS
Q. What are the 30-day, 14-day, and 90-day examination-result rules?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. Normally, results go out by the 30th day after the exam. If a testing service conducted/graded/reviewed it, notice goes out by the 14th day after the Marshal receives the results. If delay will exceed 90 days after the exam, before day 90 the examinee gets the reason. A failed examinee who requests it in writing gets a performance analysis.
Memory hook: 30 exam, 14 after PSI results, warn before 90. Write “30–14–90” three times.
Watch for: A performance analysis is not an answer key. The request must be written.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.159 · CONTINUING EDUCATION REQUIREMENTS
Q. Is continuing education mandatory under Chapter 6001?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. No. The commissioner may create/certify CE procedures and programs, but participation is voluntary.
Memory hook: CE = Can Elect.
Watch for: Trap: “may certify” and “voluntary” mean it is not a universal mandatory CE requirement in this statute.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.160 · RECIPROCAL LICENSE
Q. When may the department waive a Texas license requirement for an out-of-state applicant?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. When the applicant holds a license from another state whose licensing requirements are substantially equivalent to Texas’s requirements.
Memory hook: Reciprocal = same standards, different state.
Watch for: The department may waive. It is not automatic simply because someone has any out-of-state license.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.161 · APPRENTICE PERMIT REQUIRED
Q. What must an apprentice have, and who may directly supervise the apprentice?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. The apprentice must hold a department-issued apprentice permit. Work may be done only under direct supervision of a licensed individual who works for the same firm as the apprentice.
Memory hook: Permit + Pro + same Place.
Watch for: “Licensed person nearby” is incomplete. The supervisor must work for the same firm.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.162 · NOT TRANSFERABLE
Q. Which Chapter 6001 credentials are not transferable?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. A registration certificate, license, or permit.
Memory hook: R-L-P stays with its person or firm.
Watch for: This applies to all three; do not answer only license or only permit.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.201 · RENEWAL REQUIRED; FEE
Q. How long are registrations/licenses valid, how long is an apprentice permit valid, and when can a fee be prorated?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. Registration certificates and licenses are valid two years; fees for each year of that two-year period are paid on renewal. An apprentice permit expires on its first anniversary. The commissioner may stagger expiration dates; a first partial year may be prorated monthly.
Memory hook: 2–1–partial: license/registration two years, apprentice one, first short year can be partial/prorated.
Watch for: Do not say every renewal is prorated. After the first partial year, the total renewal fee is payable.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.202 · NOTICE OF EXPIRATION
Q. How much notice must the State Fire Marshal give before a registration certificate or license expires?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. At least 30 days before expiration, in writing, sent to the holder’s last known address.
Memory hook: 30 to the last known door.
Watch for: This provision says registration certificate or license; do not casually add apprentice permit to the notice rule.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.203 · RENEWAL PROCEDURES
Q. What are the three expired-credential renewal bands?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. 0–90 days: renewal fee + one-half of the initial fee. More than 90 days but less than 2 years: all unpaid renewal fees + the full initial fee. 2 years or longer: cannot renew; must qualify as a new applicant.
Memory hook: 90 = half; 2 = new. Imagine a half-price toll at 90, a full restart at 2 years.
Watch for: The department may still deny/refuse renewal under applicable law/rules. An unexpired credential is renewed by paying before it expires.

Prohibitions and discipline

TEX. INS. CODE §6001.251 · PROHIBITED PRACTICES
Q. What are the big prohibitions in §6001.251?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. No unregistered firm business; no unlicensed individual work; no unauthorized DOT hydro testing; no fraudulent credential application; no work that violates Chapter 6001/rules; no improper identifying labels; no carbon tetrachloride extinguisher sale/install/service/recharge; and no covered equipment without an approved listing/approval label, subject to the rule-based service exception.
Memory hook: Use the chain: Firm – Person – Hydro – Fraud – Rules – Labels – Carbon Tet – Listing.
Watch for: The famous technical trap is carbon tetrachloride, not CO₂. Also, the statute requires a rule permitting service regardless of the listing label; do not turn that narrow service exception into a broad sale/install exception.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.252 · DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS
Q. What can the State Fire Marshal do after notice and hearing for a violation, rule/standard violation, or sales/service misrepresentation?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. The Marshal may suspend, revoke, refuse to issue, or refuse to renew a registration certificate, license, or permit.
Memory hook: S-R-R-R: Suspend, Revoke, Refuse issue, Refuse renew.
Watch for: The action follows notice and hearing. “May” means authority, not an automatic penalty.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.253 · DISCIPLINARY HEARING
Q. Where is a Chapter 6001 disciplinary hearing conducted?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. At the State Office of Administrative Hearings (SOAH). Proceedings are governed by Government Code Chapter 2001, and commissioner practice rules may not conflict with SOAH rules.
Memory hook: Discipline goes to SOAH. Say “So-AH, not SFMO alone.”
Watch for: The holder is entitled to a hearing if suspension, revocation, or refusal to renew is proposed.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.254 · REAPPLICATION REQUIREMENTS
Q. After a refusal or revocation, when may someone reapply?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. Other than failure to pass a required written exam, the person must wait until the first anniversary of the effective refusal/revocation. After that, they may reapply and show good cause in a public hearing that issuance is not against public safety and welfare.
Memory hook: Revoked? One-year roadblock. Failed exam? Different road.
Watch for: The explicit exception is failure to pass a required written examination. Do not apply the one-year bar to ordinary exam failure.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.255 · REEXAMINATION AFTER REVOCATION
Q. What must a licensee do after a service/install license has been revoked before a new license may issue?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. The individual must retake and pass the required written examination.
Memory hook: Revoked = re-test.
Watch for: This is not the same as the §6001.254 waiting rule; learn them as a pair.
TEX. INS. CODE §6001.301 · CRIMINAL PENALTY
Q. What is the criminal penalty for knowingly violating §6001.251(a), and where may it be prosecuted?
Reveal answer, memory hook, and trap
A. It is a Class B misdemeanor. Venue is Travis County or the county where the offense occurred.
Memory hook: B = two places: the county of the breach or Travis.
Watch for: The word knowingly matters. This statute points specifically to §6001.251(a).
